emca — a text window.

The surface opened this file over the namespace and is editing it with a real
editor component. Everything you can do here — caret, selection, clipboard,
wrapping, find — belongs to the host and none of it was written for IPNX.

What crosses back is the buffer as insert/delete with a sequence number and a
hash, the selection, and the verbs. Right-click for those.

Cmd-S, or Put, streams this file back.
