Third-party notices

The demo serves binaries built from these sources; full notices and provenance travel with the sources in the repository.

  • Plan 9 from Bell Labs (4th edition) — the kernel design, rc, sam, acme, the libraries and commands. MIT licence, by the Plan 9 Foundation's transfer (March 2021).
  • Research Unix V10 — /v10/bin's cat and echo, built from TUHS-tape sources under the Alcatel-Lucent / Nokia statement covering Research Unix editions 8–10.
  • CPython 3.14 — the official WASI build, under the PSF License.
  • Go — the GOOS=wasip1 test binary includes the Go runtime, under the BSD-style Go licence.
  • wasi-libc — the wasi-libc citizen is built with wasi-libc (Apache-2.0 WITH LLVM-exception / MIT).

ipnx-v12 itself is MIT — inherited rather than chosen, as its README explains.
